Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what information TrueQuote Homes collects when you visit truequotehomes.com, and how it is used.

What we collect

When you visit this site, standard technical information may be collected automatically — things like your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, and the links you click. This site does not have forms and does not ask for your name, email, or phone number.

Cookies and advertising pixels

We use cookies and advertising pixels (such as the Meta Pixel) to measure how well our advertising works and to understand how visitors use the site. If you arrive here from an ad, the link may include an advertising click ID that helps with that measurement. This data may be shared with the advertising platform that showed you the ad, which handles it under its own privacy policy.

Third-party sites

When you pick a service on this site, you are taken to an independent quote marketplace that we do not operate. Anything you enter there — your name, address, email, phone — is collected by that site under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to read before submitting. We are not responsible for other sites' privacy practices.

How information is used

We use the information described above to run and improve the site, measure our advertising, and connect you to relevant quote services. We do not sell your name, phone number, or email — this site doesn't collect them in the first place.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings — the site works fine without them.
  • You can opt out of interest-based ads at aboutads.info/choices, or manage Facebook ad preferences at facebook.com/adpreferences.
  • You can email us at hello@truequotehomes.com with any privacy question or request — including requests to access or delete data — and we'll respond promptly.

Children

This site is for homeowners and intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new date.
